Background

Practitioner.
Researcher.
Advocate.


I came to social work the long way — through lived experience with housing instability, family disruption, and navigating systems that weren't designed to help people like me. That path didn't disqualify me from this field. It made me better at it.

At 45, I'm completing my BSW at Southern Illinois University Carbondale and beginning an Advanced Standing MSW at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign in Fall 2026. My academic focus sits at the intersection of clinical practice, community advocacy, and research on structural barriers — the systems that interrupt informal survival strategies without replacing them with viable alternatives.

"Trauma precedes dysfunction. Systems create invisibility. My work is about closing that gap."

I've served as an elected board representative for NASW-Illinois, completed a field practicum building regional resource infrastructure for Parkinson's Disease caregivers, and co-own Illinois Senior Benefit Services — a life and health insurance agency serving seniors across Southern Illinois. Each of those roles, different as they look, is the same work: connecting people to what they need.

I'm a nontraditional student, a father, a husband, and a storyteller. My experience in CDL instruction, entrepreneurship, and community organizing all feed into how I understand systems — and how I practice within them.

Research & Projects
01
Victimology · CCJ 462

Institutional Vulnerability: How the Foster Care System Produces Victims of Sex Trafficking

Applied Routine Activity Theory to examine structural conditions within foster care that create opportunity for trafficking victimization. Identified gaps in RAT's explanatory power for youth who lack guardianship autonomy, and proposed supplementary frameworks grounded in structural choice theory.

02
Research Methods · Dr. Solimon

Student Perception of Risky Behavior at SIU: A Quantitative Study

Led a group research project examining risky behavior perceptions among SIU students (N=47). Ran Pearson correlations and independent-samples t-tests using SPSS. Produced a complete academic paper with publication-quality statistical charts and data visualization.

03
Field Practicum · SOCW 443

Parkinson's Disease Regional Resource Toolkit — Southern Illinois

Built a comprehensive 53-entry provider directory and adaptive equipment resource guide for Parkinson's patients and caregivers across a three-state service region. Conducted vendor outreach, documented service gaps, and produced formatted appendices for clinical handoff to Dr. Jurkowski's team.

04
Narrative Nonfiction · In Development

On Ramp: Stories from the Outer Road

A narrative nonfiction book project organized around structural barriers to mobility — housing, systems, recovery, and reentry. Written from lived experience and academic grounding in social work theory. Explores the space between where systems leave people and where they actually end up.
