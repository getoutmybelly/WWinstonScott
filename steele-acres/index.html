A little place with a lot of possibilities

Room to breathe. Close to the lake.

Tucked among mature trees just south of Marion, Steele Acres is an affordable Southern Illinois property for someone who wants outdoor space, a quieter setting, and convenient access to the Lake of Egypt area and the region's parks, forests, trails, fishing and recreation.

The property includes a 2-bedroom, 1-bath home, a large fenced yard, gravel parking, mature trees and a separate outbuilding. It is offered directly by the owner.

Verified real-property status

The manufactured home was previously converted to real property and is part of the real estate. Because of that conversion, there is no separate manufactured-home/mobile-home certificate of title. The property transfers through the normal real-estate transaction and deed/title process.
