# Michelle's Funding Finder

Live GitHub Pages path (when Pages serves this repository):
https://getoutmybelly.github.io/WWinstonScott/michelle-funding-finder/

A mobile-friendly, no-login funding opportunity dashboard for Michelle. It tracks:
- Grants for women-owned and small businesses
- Real estate and business education scholarships
- Business loans and fixed-asset financing (clearly distinguished from grants)
- Illinois and national funding directories
- Eligibility, official source links, application stages, personal notes, and deadlines

## Using it

1. Open the page on a phone or computer.
2. Use Explore opportunities to browse or filter starter leads.
3. Tap the heart to add a lead to My funding pipeline.
4. Choose a stage (researching, preparing, applied, awarded) and add notes.
5. Use Add an opportunity to enter new leads from any reliable source.
6. Open Find new funding for trusted official directories.
7. On My funding pipeline, export CSV, download a deadline calendar (.ics), or back up/restore JSON.

## Important limitations

This version is a static GitHub Pages site. It does **not** scrape funding sources, send scheduled email alerts, or automatically update the provider catalog. The included leads were researched on **October 18, 2026**; always re-check the organization link for current deadlines, amounts, fees, qualifications and open/closed status.

The user's favorites, stages, notes and custom leads are saved only in the device/browser's localStorage. Changes do not automatically synchronize across devices or other users. Use **Back up my tracker** and **Restore backup** to migrate them. The public repository contains only the app and published starter leads, not private user entries.
