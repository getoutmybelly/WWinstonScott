Research fit

The emerging through-line is not one topic. It is a recurring question about what happens when systems offer a solution but leave structural gaps around the people expected to use it.

Financial vulnerability + life insurance

Economic precarity, intergenerational vulnerability, financial capability, ethics, and whether products marketed as protection actually improve long-term household security.

Social + economic justice

Jane Addams is especially strong as a home for research that frames financial vulnerability, service access, and institutional design as social-justice and policy questions.

AI + human services

Digital mental-health tools, ethical AI, intervention design, accessibility, and evaluation of technology used with vulnerable populations.

Bariatric aftermath + continuity of care

Post-operative guidance, long-term psychosocial adjustment, quality of follow-up, informal advice ecosystems, and gaps between surgery and sustained support.

Application plan

October 2026 · Turn interest into evidence

Follow up on the Illinois PhD-pipeline conversation. Identify one realistic research-assistant, faculty-project, evaluation, conference, or Skybound research-development opportunity.

October–November · Build three faculty-fit maps

Identify 2–4 faculty at UIUC, UIC, and Iowa whose current work could plausibly support the research questions. Do not write essays around school reputation; write them around mentorship and research environment.

November · Convert coursework into a writing sample

Choose the strongest research/evaluation paper and revise it beyond the class requirement so it reads like evidence of doctoral potential.

By December 1 · Illinois-ready

Have the Illinois packet essentially finished even if the official Fall 2027 deadline is later.

By December 15 · UIC early deadline

Submit Jane Addams by the early deadline to preserve eligibility for University Fellowship nomination.

By February 1 · Iowa

Submit Iowa after incorporating what was learned from the Illinois and UIC applications.

Spring 2027 · Compare offers, mentors, and money

Do not choose based on school name. Compare guaranteed funding, mentor availability, RA opportunities, dissertation fit, family logistics, and what each program would let Winston build.

Current recommendation

Apply to all three. Build toward Illinois first. Take UIC very seriously.

Illinois still leads because Winston already has access to faculty who can watch him develop and because the PhD conversation has begun organically. UIC Jane Addams is now the strongest external challenger: its mission aligns directly with the structural justice questions Winston keeps returning to, it has a clear December 15 early deadline, and its best fellowships can include four years of roughly $30,000 annual support plus tuition. Iowa remains a strong third application because its interdisciplinary and AI/economic-hardship fit is distinctive.

The smartest move is not to decide now. The smartest move is to create three strong applications and let admissions, mentor fit, and guaranteed funding create the real decision in spring.
