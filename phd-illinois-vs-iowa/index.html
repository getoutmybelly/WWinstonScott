Funding changes the board

For a research doctorate, the offer package is part of the academic decision. A strong mentor fit without sustainable funding is not automatically the better option.

WashU: strongest published guarantee

All admitted Social Work PhD students receive five years of tuition coverage and stipend support. The published 2026–27 base stipend is $37,853/year, plus $1,000/year for professional development. The stipend is not tied to work responsibilities.

Mizzou: unusually clear package

The Social Work PhD publishes support for up to four years: monthly stipend while serving as a GRA/GTA, full tuition waiver, and health-insurance coverage. University doctoral assistantship minimums for 2026–27 begin at $20,392 for nine months or $27,192 for 12 months, though actual program offers may be higher.

UIC: fellowship upside

UIC says its most competitive university fellowships provide approximately $30,000/year for four years plus tuition waiver. The application must be in by Dec. 15 for fellowship nomination. Jane Addams RA appointments can also include stipend and tuition waiver.

UIUC: relationship advantage, package needs confirmation

Illinois offers PhD scholarships and doctoral support opportunities, but the public admissions materials are less explicit about a universal multi-year package. If admitted, compare the actual funding letter carefully against WashU, Mizzou, UIC, and Iowa.

Iowa: funded admits can receive full support

Iowa states that, when funds are available, full-time doctoral students offered funding with admission receive priority for assistantships that can include tuition, stipend, and health insurance. Confirm the specific package in the offer.

How your research agenda maps

The strongest application story is not “I have four unrelated interests.” It is a common question about what happens when institutions offer a solution but the surrounding system leaves people exposed.

Economic vulnerability · insurance · financial capability

WashU: unusually deep history in financial capability, asset building, household economic security, and social/economic development through the Brown School and Center for Social Development.

UIUC: Dr. Okumu's financial-capability work gives this question an existing faculty doorway.

UIC: strong justice/policy framing for studying how markets and systems affect vulnerable urban populations.

AI · digital mental health · Skybound

UIUC: strongest immediate development pathway because Skybound has already been seen and discussed locally.

Iowa: meaningful alignment around ethical AI, economic hardship, and digital mental-health support.

Mizzou: human-service information systems and intervention research create a systems-oriented route.

Bariatric surgery · aftercare · guidance quality

WashU: broad health-disparities and health-services research ecosystem may support an interdisciplinary version of this question.

UIUC/UIC: both provide pathways to frame post-surgical guidance as an access, continuity-of-care, or intervention-design problem.

Mizzou: health-risk behavior and clinical/practice research could offer an applied route.

Program evaluation · vulnerable populations · systems

Mizzou: explicitly emphasizes theory-driven, community-based collaborative research and program/policy evaluation.

UIUC: you are already building evaluation experience and relationships in the environment where you would apply.

WashU: exceptionally strong methods and policy-impact infrastructure.

Application sequence

Now · October 2026

Turn the broad agenda into 1–2 research questions. Ask Dr. Okumu what a serious PhD-bound MSW student should do this semester to demonstrate research potential.

October–November

Identify 2–3 faculty at each school. Read recent papers. Build one reusable research statement, then customize the mentor/program-fit section for each institution.

By mid-November

Lock three recommenders and give them a short research memo, CV, deadlines, and the specific programs.

December 1

Target UIUC, WashU, and Mizzou. Even where UIUC's direct page has not yet updated, Dec. 1 is the safe internal deadline.

December 15

Submit UIC Jane Addams early so you remain eligible for university fellowship nomination.

February 1, 2027

Submit Iowa no later than its published Fall 2027 deadline.

Offer season

Compare mentor fit, guaranteed years, stipend, tuition/fees, health insurance, research obligations, relocation, and what each school can do for the research agenda — not prestige alone.

Current recommendation

Apply to all five.

Build toward UIUC first because it is the one place where faculty can watch your development before the admissions committee sees the file. Do not treat WashU as a fantasy application: its Brown School belongs on the serious list, especially given the financial-capability thread and the guaranteed funding. Mizzou belongs above “backup” status because its four-year support package and community/program-evaluation orientation are genuinely compatible with your work. UIC remains a strong justice-centered option, and Iowa remains a solid interdisciplinary choice with the latest deadline.

The application thesis should be consistent across all five: you want to study how helping systems, interventions, and financial or health products can appear to solve a problem while leaving structural gaps that continue to expose vulnerable people — and how better evaluation and intervention design can close those gaps.
