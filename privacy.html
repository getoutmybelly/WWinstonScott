Field Notes Publisher Privacy Policy

Effective August 4, 2026

Plain-language summary: Field Notes Publisher helps W. Winston Scott prepare and publish content he has reviewed and approved. It does not sell personal information or publish without an explicit approval.

What this application does

Field Notes Publisher is a private editorial and scheduling application operated by W. Winston Scott. It connects to authorized social-media accounts so approved drafts can be published at the time selected by the account owner.

Information the application may access

When the account owner authorizes LinkedIn, the application may receive the basic account identifier and permissions necessary to publish approved content. It may also store:

  • draft and approved post text;
  • optional links or media selected for a post;
  • approval and scheduling information; and
  • the identifier or link returned after a post is published.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the account owner, maintain the private editorial queue, publish owner-approved content, and record whether a requested publication succeeded.

Human approval

The application is designed to keep publication decisions under human control. Draft generation, research, or scheduling does not by itself authorize publication. A post must be affirmatively approved before it can be sent to LinkedIn.

Sharing and sale of information

The application does not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers required to operate the application, such as hosting infrastructure and LinkedIn, and only as needed to complete an authorized function.

Retention and security

Application credentials are stored in protected server-side environment settings rather than public website code. Drafts, scheduling records, and publication receipts are retained only for operational and editorial-history purposes and may be deleted when no longer needed.

Revoking access or requesting deletion

The account owner may revoke LinkedIn access through LinkedIn account settings. Requests to review or delete application-held information may be sent to wwinstonscott@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application’s capabilities or connected services change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
